Frequently Asked Questions

You need to update two files with your AdMob IDs:

  1. Info.plist: Update the GADApplicationIdentifier with your AdMob App ID
  2. Constants.swift: Update the rewardedAdUnitID with your Ad Unit ID

Find detailed instructions in our AdMob Setup Guide.

Common causes and solutions:

  • Wrong IDs: Double-check your AdMob App ID and Ad Unit ID
  • Account not approved: Wait 24-48 hours after creating AdMob account
  • Testing location: Ads may not be available in all regions
  • Internet connection: Ensure device has stable internet

See our Troubleshooting Guide for more details.

Speech recognition only works on physical devices, not simulators. Make sure:

  • You're testing on a real iPhone or iPad
  • Microphone permissions are granted
  • The video has clear, audible speech
  • Selected language is supported

To customize your app's appearance:

  1. App Icon: Replace files in Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/
  2. Colors: Update the Colors struct in Constants.swift
  3. App Name: Change Display Name in Xcode project settings

Full instructions in our Customization Guide.
