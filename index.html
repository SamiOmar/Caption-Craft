Frequently Asked Questions

Which video formats are supported?

Caption Craft supports all common video formats like MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and M4V. Videos can be up to 10 minutes long and up to 500MB in size.

How accurate is the automatic speech recognition?

Our AI-powered speech recognition achieves over 95% accuracy with clear audio quality. Accuracy may vary depending on background noise, accent, and speaking speed.

Are my videos uploaded to the cloud?

No, all processing happens locally on your iPhone. Your videos never leave your device, ensuring maximum privacy and security.

Can I manually edit the subtitles?

Yes, you can edit all automatically created subtitles, correct them, or add new ones. Timing, text, and styling are fully customizable.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Caption Craft is completely free to use with all features included. If you have any issues, please contact our support team.
